# Check-in questions (for the EM's own survey tool)

Turn off name and email collection. Make every question optional. Scale: 1 strongly disagree to 5 strongly agree.

1. **Workload:** My workload over the last month has been manageable.
2. **Control:** I have a say in how I do my work.
3. **Reward:** My work is recognised in a way that matters to me.
4. **Community:** I feel supported by the people I work with.
5. **Fairness:** Decisions on this team are made fairly.
6. **Values:** The work we do fits what I care about.
7. **Optional:** Anything you want to add? (Free text. Avoid details that identify you.)

Export columns: period (or submitted_at), workload, control, reward, community, fairness, values, comment.
