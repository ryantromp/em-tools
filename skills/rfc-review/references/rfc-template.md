# Default RFC template

Used when the team has none. Sections in rough order of what reviewers need.

1. **Summary:** what changes, in 2 or 3 sentences.
2. **Problem:** who has the problem, the evidence, and why now.
3. **Proposal:** the design, the paths or services it touches, and data changes.
4. **Success criteria:** how we will know it worked, with numbers and a date.
5. **Rollout and rollback:** steps, flags, how to undo it, and who decides.
6. **Dependencies and stakeholders:** teams and people who must agree or act.
7. **Risks:** what could go wrong and how it is mitigated.
8. **Alternatives considered:** including "do nothing".
9. **Open questions.**
