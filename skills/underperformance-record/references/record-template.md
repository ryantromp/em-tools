## Wording checks before saving

| Category | Flag | Try instead |
|---|---|---|
| Vague | "be more proactive", "step up", "not good enough", "lacks ownership" | What would you see if it were met? |
| Personality | "lazy", "difficult", "negative", "attitude problem" | The observable behaviour, with a date |
| Coded | "not a culture fit", "abrasive", "emotional" | The behaviour and its effect on the work |
| Absolute | "always", "never", "every time" | Count instances, with dates |
| Guessing at a cause | "doesn't care", "checked out", "distracted" | Leave it out; record only what happened |
| Verdict | "low performer", "PIP candidate", "won't improve" | Leave it out; the record holds facts |
