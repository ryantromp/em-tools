# Generic engineering ladder (used when the team has none)

A neutral, five-competency ladder for evidence mapping only. Replace it with your organisation's ladder by adding `ladder.md` to your notes folder and naming it in `team.md`.

| Competency | Engineer | Senior Engineer | Staff Engineer |
|---|---|---|---|
| execution | Delivers well-scoped tasks with tests and clear status | Delivers multi-week projects predictably; breaks work down for others | Delivers initiatives that span several teams |
| technical-design | Works within existing designs; asks good questions | Designs systems for the team; writes and reviews design docs | Sets technical direction across teams; resolves trade-offs |
| collaboration | Communicates clearly with the team | Aligns with partner teams and stakeholders | Builds agreement across the organisation |
| mentoring | Helps peers through code review and pairing | Mentors engineers on the team; improves onboarding | Grows senior engineers and leads |
| ownership | Owns own tasks end to end | Owns a service or area, including incidents | Owns outcomes across services; improves how teams work |
