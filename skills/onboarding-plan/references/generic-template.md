# Generic onboarding template

Used when the team has no `onboarding.md`. Adapt to the role and level.

## Before day 1
- Accounts, access and equipment ready.
- Buddy named and introduced.
- First-week calendar: team rituals, 1:1, buddy sessions.

## Days 1 to 30: learn and set up
- Local setup working; can run the tests.
- First small change merged with a reviewer from the team.
- Has met the people they will work with most.
- Knows how work is planned and where to ask for help.

## Days 31 to 60: contribute
- Owns a small, scoped change end to end.
- Shadows on-call or a release before joining any rotation.
- Shares one thing they learned with the team.

## Days 61 to 90: own
- Owns an area, a service or a stream of work, with the buddy as backup.
- Joins a rotation if they feel ready.
- Agrees career goals for the next six months.

## First 1:1 questions (Lara Hogan)
- How do you like to receive feedback, and recognition?
- What does a great manager do for you? What has a bad one done?
- What are you most excited to learn? What worries you?
- How do you work best, and what gets in the way?

## Check-in questions
- What has been easier or harder than you expected?
- What do you wish you had known in week one?
- Where do you feel blocked, and who could help?
- Which milestone should we change?

## Buddy checklist
- Week 1: pair on setup and the first change; introduce three people.
- Week 2: review a change together; explain how on-call works.
- Week 4: a 30-minute check-in on what is still confusing.
